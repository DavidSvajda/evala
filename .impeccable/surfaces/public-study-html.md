# Evala web app

Mode: Operate. Beginners need a next task, explanations and approachable puzzles; experienced players need saved analysis, notation and MultiPV without friction. User selected cream/green, persistent navigation and Google sign-in. The requested familiar chess application language takes precedence over the random concept assignment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal chess workspace connecting the player's games to a concrete training queue. Separate addressable pages for overview, games, analysis, puzzles, insights and settings.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk cream surfaces, forest green actions, sage and ivory squares, charcoal text. Self-hosted Manrope. Flat table rows, restrained separators, 8px controls, generous space around a large playable board. No marketing slogans or ornamental statistics.

STORY: Import a game, review its critical positions, practise a verified tactic, return to mistakes when due. Guest data stays local; signed-in players sync an isolated personal library and training history.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 212px left navigation rail. Overview places a real playable puzzle preview beside a short training queue, with recent games beneath. Analysis gives most width to the board and notation. Puzzles show a large board, side to move, move feedback and next action. On mobile the rail becomes a compact navigation strip. Signature interaction: a legal move advances the puzzle and plays its reply; failed moves stay available for correction.

FORM: User-pinned familiar chess workspace; grounded candidate 6, club training notebook. Seed 578e15d5 acknowledged; cream/green and standard section navigation explicitly chosen by the user override catalog challengers. Code-led functional board and native controls; no decorative raster artwork.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
