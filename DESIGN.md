# Evala visual system

User-pinned compact vertical bar beside the board. Operate mode: read evaluation without opening a panel.

The interface borrows from quiet chess clocks: graphite #191b1a, warm white #f2f0e9, muted brass #d5b77a, secondary text #b2b6ad. A clear midpoint marker separates neutral from changing advantage. The white segment grows for positive scores, the black segment for negative scores. Orientation follows the board; score always remains from White's perspective.

Manrope is bundled locally. Tabular numerals for scores, depth and speed. Small SVG stroke icons, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support. Settings use one vertical form, restrained separators and no decorative cards. The extension bar has no persistent wide panel. Controls live in the popup or an anchored settings popover.

The study surface is Operate mode in the same visual world: narrow library at left, a primary interactive board and actual evaluation graph in the center, and review controls, results, lines and move notation at right. On narrow screens the library is toggled and the results follow the board vertically. Imported user content drives all player names, dates and results. No fabricated benchmark or official Chess.com badge claims.
