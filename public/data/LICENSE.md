# Lichess puzzles

Source: https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles

The Lichess database exports are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Evala bundles a rating-stratified subset of 4,459 original puzzles. Original puzzle IDs, ratings, themes, solution moves and source game URLs are preserved. Preparation script: scripts/prepare-puzzles.py. The initial exported move is the opponent's setup move and is played before the puzzle begins.
